# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in Inertia Localize to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive. Treat people with patience and respect regardless of experience, identity, background, or point of view.

Examples of behavior that supports this environment include using considerate language, accepting constructive feedback, focusing criticism on ideas and code, and helping others learn.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insults, discrimination, sexualized attention, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, and publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project-related communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report harmful behavior privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not report it in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality as far as practical, and may remove content or restrict participation. Maintainers are expected to apply these rules consistently and explain enforcement decisions when appropriate.

## Maintainer responsibility

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying contribution expectations, reviewing reports, and taking fair, proportionate action. Maintainers are held to the same standard as other participants.
